- ✅ **Free to reuse**: values (colours, spacing, radii, easing) and open-source fonts and icons. Install them from their official source.

## Reference files (`reference/`)

88 file(s): exact copies of what the source page used. Its markup and stylesheets (the text replaced by filler of the same length), its SVG drawings, pictures, font files and animation data, kept so an agent can reproduce the page faithfully instead of approximating it. They still belong to the source site's owner. Use them where you have the right to: your own site, a licensed template, or a client who gave you the files. Otherwise keep the *look* and replace the files. A font marked 🔑 above needs a licence even though its file is here.

Frames in `frames/` have images replaced by flat blocks and text replaced by bars. Apart from `reference/`, this kit contains no font files, images, logos, stylesheets or text from the source site.
