# Voice

Tone: concise, direct, energetic, formal.

- Keep sentences around 8 words; that is the measured average across the site's copy.
- Write headings in Title Case, as 13 of 14 measured headings are.
- Address the reader as "you" (about 2.4 times per 100 words on the site).
- Exclamation marks appear in the copy; use them sparingly for real moments of energy.
- Label buttons with a verb first, about 2 words long.

## Examples (new sentences in this voice)

- **headline**: Plan less. Ship more.
- **button**: Start now
- **empty state**: Nothing here yet.
- **error**: That didn't work. Try again.
- **headline**: Your best work starts today!
- **button**: Let's go
